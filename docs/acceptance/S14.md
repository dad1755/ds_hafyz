## Verdict

**FAIL** at `176564e`, on clause part 3 only.

Four of the clause's five parts hold under this gate's own execution: the 26 components are mapped and
all have numbered drafts; the 18 rubric items are accounted for (9+9, 20 marks each) against evidence
that exists; there is no unsupported `PASS` (all 14 trace to acceptance records that carry the verdict
themselves) and no `completed` at all; and no group contribution or date is fabricated — the 14-day
schedule and the four-member division are the two claims marked `unconfirmed`, and the report leaves
them as placeholders rather than filling them.

The traceability part fails twice, and both are the failure mode this sprint exists to prevent: a
sentence claiming exactness that the cited file denies. `13_comparison.md:40` (and its echo at
`report_evidence.md:661`) says the v3 Table 9 figures "reproduce to four decimals" while
`report_evidence.md` §7.3 — cited in the same sentence — records fourth-decimal deltas for the Decision
Tree and states plainly that it "does not reproduce exactly". `report_evidence.md:726` credits a
committed artefact with "312 files scanned" where the artefact says 292.

Both are one-line corrections, and the correct wording already exists elsewhere in the same file
(`report_evidence.md:171`), so the fix is a correction of two summaries, not new analysis. This gate did
not make them: the card is read-only on the drafts, and a gate that edits what it grades has no verdict
left to give. The card stays `in_review` — a gate card must not go `done` on a FAIL. Re-gate after the
two edits is one command block: `check_report_refs.py`, `check_report_numbers.py`,
`audit_s14_gate.py`.
