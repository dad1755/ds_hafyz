## 7. Defects

**D-1 (blocking) — `docs/presentation_script.md:175`: a measured figure that no artefact contains,
and that the transcript it cites contradicts.**

> "A cold Streamlit session behind the Cloudflare edge measured 32.4 s once and 5.7 s in the recorded
> run."

`5.7` appears nowhere in `evidence/s15/` or anywhere else in the repo (`grep -rn "5\.7 *s"` finds only
this line). The recorded run measures **3.6 s** first paint (`evidence/s15/demo_rehearsal.txt:24`,
`PASS sign-in form rendered — first paint 3.6s after navigation`) and **5.5 s** for step 1
(`demo_rehearsal.txt:86`, `"step": "open the live URL", "seconds": 5.5`). The 32.4 s figure is
supported — but only by `evidence/s15/README.md:45`, an earlier attempt, not by the transcript.

This is precisely the failure mode the card names: a figure in the deck that no artefact supports.
`check_script_numbers.py` cannot catch it because it only asserts that the 82 known figures are
*present*; it never looks for figures that should not be there.

*Required change:* either delete "and 5.7 s in the recorded run" (keeping 32.4 s attributed to the
earlier attempt in `README.md:45`), or state the number the transcript records (3.6 s first paint /
5.5 s for step 1). Then re-run `check_script_numbers.py` and `audit_s15_gate.py`.

**D-2 (blocking for the §9 deliverable) — the deck has no "User prediction demo" slide.**

`blueprint.md:534` fixes the slide order: "…architecture, Admin demo, User prediction demo,
ethics/limitations, conclusion/improvements". The twelve slides are 1 problem, 2 objectives,
3 dataset, 4 preprocessing, 5 EDA, 6 methodology, 7 comparison, 8 confusion matrices, 9 architecture,
10 *Admin module*, 11 ethics, 12 conclusion. Slide 10 is admin-only; the user prediction journey
exists only in the demo script (§15.3 steps 7–10), not as a slide. §10 scores "User prediction demo"
at **3 marks**, and §9 names S15 as one of its evidencing sprints.

*Required change:* add a "User prediction demo" slide — the 23-input form in its four groups, the
scored result with probability/band/threshold/model version, the refused invalid input, and the
user's own history — and merge slides 7 and 8 (comparison table + confusion matrices) if the 12-slide
ceiling matters, since the blueprint allows 10–12 slides.
